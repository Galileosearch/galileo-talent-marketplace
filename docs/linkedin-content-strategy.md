## Strategic Framework

### The Two Audiences (and why the sequencing matters)

You're building a two-sided marketplace. The LinkedIn strategy needs to warm up both sides, but in the right order:

**Weeks 1–2: Lead with the PROBLEM (attracts clients)**
- Establish Galileo Search as the company that understands the enterprise AI adoption challenge
- Don't sell the marketplace yet — sell the insight
- Client-side executives see these posts and think "these people get it"

**Weeks 3–4: Introduce the SOLUTION (attracts talent + clients)**
- Now reveal what you're building and why
- Talent sees the opportunity and starts paying attention
- Clients see a credible answer to the problems you've been articulating

### Why This Order Works
If you launch with "we're building a talent marketplace" — nobody cares. There's no context.
If you spend 2 weeks demonstrating you deeply understand the problem, then reveal you're building the solution — people are already nodding before you pitch.

### Posting Cadence
- **3 posts per week** from the company page (Mon / Wed / Fri)
- Monday: Thought leadership / industry insight
- Wednesday: Practical / educational content
- Friday: Behind-the-scenes / community building
- **12 posts total over 4 weeks**

### Tone
- Direct, no corporate waffle
- Australian voice — confident but not arrogant
- Data where possible, opinions where useful
- No buzzword bingo — if you say "AI transformation" make it specific
